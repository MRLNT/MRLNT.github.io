FAQ

My purchase didn't arrive. / Pembelian tidak masuk.
Open the Store and tap restore purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID.
Buka Toko lalu ketuk pulihkan pembelian. Pembelian terikat ke Apple ID-mu.

Does the game need internet? / Perlu internet?
No. Without internet the game is fully playable; only ads and ad rewards are hidden.
Tidak. Tanpa internet game tetap bisa dimainkan penuh; hanya iklan & hadiah iklan yang disembunyikan.

Where is my progress saved? / Progres disimpan di mana?
On your device only. Deleting the app deletes your progress.
Hanya di perangkatmu. Menghapus aplikasi akan menghapus progres.

What are the chest odds? / Berapa peluang peti?
The odds and guarantee (pity) are shown in the game before every purchase or opening, and every item can also be earned for free.
Peluang & jaminan (pity) ditampilkan di game sebelum membeli/membuka, dan semua isi juga bisa didapat gratis.
